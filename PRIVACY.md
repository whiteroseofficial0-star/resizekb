# Privacy
ResizeKB processes images and PDFs entirely in your browser. Files are never uploaded, stored or logged. The only data kept is your saved presets and last-used settings, in your browser's localStorage. Not affiliated with any government or exam body.

Two decorative assets load from third-party CDNs: the hero video (MotionSite / DigitalOcean Spaces) and the Playfair Display and Inter webfonts (Google Fonts). Those requests reveal your IP address and browser details to those hosts, as any font or video request does, but they never include your images or any file you open in the tool. Block them and the page still works with system fonts and a CSS gradient hero.
